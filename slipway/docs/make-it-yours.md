# Make it yours

This is our process with our names taken out. Keep your own taste, pace and tools in your own repo,
link back here, and send fixes back so everyone gets them.

Your version needs one page that lists the five phases and says only what you do differently —
where ideas come from, your design kit, your build wrapper, your hosting, your tracker. Link here
for the rest; don't copy the phase pages. Add one line on naming: your studio name, how apps are
named, and any old names that should never show up in public again.

Keep your catalog at the top of your apps folder, and an `app.json` in every app. Add fields if you
need them; the checker ignores what it doesn't know. Receipts, the review contact, where the key
lives and device IDs go somewhere private, never in a public repo.

Write your defaults down once: release type, territories, privacy stance, the one sentence about
website analytics, the support address.

Then decide a few things early. How fast you submit, and your rule for two apps of the same kind.
What the 30- and 90-day numbers mean for folding an app into another or retiring it. Who the human
is at each phase. And what's public — we build in public, but unpicked ideas and anything personal
stay out.

When Apple changes something, fix it here, keep the date and the link next to the claim, and bump
the status date in the README.
