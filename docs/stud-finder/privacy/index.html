Stud Finder: Facedown for iPhone

Privacy policy

Stud Finder: Facedown contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, makes no network requests and has no Open AppCo server. It does not collect any information about you or how you use it. It does not use the camera, microphone or location.

On your iPhone

The app reads the magnetometer and motion sensors only while a scan is running, and stores its settings and the sensor spot you fine-tune in its own container on your iPhone. It also keeps a short record of the last scan (the strongest reading and how many vibrations played) on the iPhone to help with support. Nothing leaves the device. Deleting the app removes its data; iOS backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings.

Help websites

Support and Privacy links open your browser. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and receives technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement. The Open AppCo website uses PostHog pageview, click analytics and masked session replay; these website tools are not embedded in the iPhone app.

Use support@openappco.com or Open AppCo support for questions. GitHub issues are public.
