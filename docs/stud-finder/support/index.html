Stud Finder: Facedown for iPhone

Help with Stud Finder

Stud Finder: Facedown feels for the steel screws and nails that hold drywall to the studs, using your iPhone's magnetometer. You feel the result through vibration. This version has no account, ads or in-app purchases.

Find a stud

Take off any magnetic case or MagSafe wallet. Tap Start, put the phone screen side down on the wall and slide it slowly sideways. A light tick about once a second means it is looking. Near a screw or nail the ticks speed up and get stronger, with two strong thumps right over it. Lift the phone to see how sure it is: Likely stud, Maybe or Probably not. Put it back on the wall to keep going, or tap Stop.

What it can't find

It finds metal only. It can't see wood, wires or pipes. Before you drill, check for wires and pipes another way.

Where the sensor is

The sensor is a small part inside your iPhone, and the metal has to pass under it. On iPhone 17 Pro the app marks the spot: the top right as you look at the back. On other iPhones it is near the top. To pin it down, tap (i), then Fine-tune sensor spot: lay the phone screen up, hold a screw or key against your fingertip and slide it slowly over the screen for 15 seconds.

Sound

Sound is off at first. In (i), turn on Geiger clicks to hear a click with each vibration.

Magnet warning

A long buzz and the words "Too strong" mean a magnet is close to the sensor. Take off magnetic cases, wallets and grips and try again.

Accessibility

The whole job is felt through vibration, so it works the same with the screen against the wall and with VoiceOver, which reads the steps and the result on screen. Larger Text is supported.
