Brush Along for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV

Support

Brush Along shows a silent two minute brushing guide in Picture in Picture, so it can float over another video app. It has no account, ads or in-app purchases. If something here doesn't answer your question, email studio@openappco.com.

Questions

Picture in Picture doesn't start on Apple TV

Press Start again. If the screen says Picture in Picture is not available, make sure the Apple TV is on tvOS 17 or later. Once the guide is floating, double-click the TV button on the remote and open your video app. The guide stays in the corner.

There's no sound

That's on purpose. The floating guide is silent so your child's show keeps its own sound. Everything the guide needs to say is on screen. On iPhone and iPad, the full screen coach has spoken instructions you can turn on or off.

The guide stopped when I closed the window

Closing the Picture in Picture window pauses the guide. Open Brush Along and press Start to begin again from the countdown. At the end of the two minutes the window closes by itself.

Which teeth does it start with?

Top right, top left, bottom left, bottom right. In each quarter it shows the outside for 12 seconds, the inside for 12 seconds and the chewing surfaces for 6. The picture is a mirror view, so "your right" matches what your child sees in a mirror.

Do I need to buy it more than once?

No. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Apple TV when they use the same Apple Account. People in your Family Sharing group can download it too.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles purchases and refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose Brush Along.

Is this medical advice?

No. The timing is a teaching rhythm and the animation has not been reviewed by a dentist. A grown-up should help young children brush and check the back teeth. Follow your dentist's advice and the instructions for your toothbrush.
