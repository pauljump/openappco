Support

A quiet brushing guide for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. Setup and purchases are for the grown-up.

Watch on this iPhone or iPad

  1. Launch Open Brush and choose Watch on this iPhone or iPad. An Apple TV is not required.
  2. Choose Full Screen to focus on the guide, or Picture in Picture to float it beside a compatible video app.
  3. For Picture in Picture, switch to your video app and press Play if needed. The guide gives you ten seconds before brushing starts.

Tap the full-screen video to show playback controls. Pause whenever you need a break. Close Full Screen with the × button; close a floating guide with its own × button. Practice & grown-up tips lets you replay a single surface with optional spoken instructions.

Start directly on Apple TV

  1. Install and open Open Brush on your Apple TV box.
  2. Choose Full Screen, or Picture in Picture to watch beside your show.
  3. After Picture in Picture starts, double-click the remote’s TV button, select your video app, and press Play if needed.

In Full Screen, press Play/Pause to pause and Back to return. Manage the floating guide with Apple TV’s Picture in Picture controls. It closes when the routine ends.

Set up iPhone control of Apple TV

  1. Install Open Brush on your iPhone and on your Apple TV box. Open the TV app once.
  2. Use the same Apple Account for purchases on both devices. Activate the $0.00 1-day trial or buy the lifetime unlock, then choose Restore Purchases on the other device if needed.
  3. On the phone, choose Set up my Apple TV. Keep both devices on the same home network and allow Local Network access when asked.
  4. Select your TV and enter the four-digit code shown on it. This is a pairing code, not your Apple Account password.
  5. Start a show on the TV, then tap Start on Apple TV on your phone. Keep the phone app open while the guide starts and your show returns. Confirm that you can see both playing.

Set up once; next time, play your show and use Start on Apple TV. TV control requires an Apple TV streaming box with the App Store. The Apple TV app built into a smart TV is not enough. Your toothbrush does not pair with the app.

How long is the guide?

The full film lasts 2 minutes 22 seconds: 10 seconds to get ready, 120 seconds on teeth, 8 extra seconds for a gentle tongue clean, and a 4-second finish card. The six tooth sections are top outside, bottom outside, top inside, bottom inside, top chewing, and bottom chewing. Each outside or inside section lasts 24 seconds; each chewing section lasts 12 seconds.

Follow the words and the brush with a light touch. Stop tongue cleaning if uncomfortable. A grown-up should help and check missed spots. The sequence is a teaching rhythm, not proof that teeth are clean.

Trial and purchases

The $0.00 1-day trial starts only when a grown-up activates “1-day Trial” through Apple. It lasts 24 hours and does not charge you automatically. After it ends, starting another guide requires the separate US $2.99 lifetime unlock. This is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Setup and help remain available, and a guide already playing can finish.

Use the same purchasing Apple Account on your iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. Choose Trial & purchases → Restore Purchases on the other device if access has not appeared. Reinstalling does not restart the trial. Family Sharing is not enabled. Apple handles payments and refund requests.

Only the older FamilyBeta23 internal build bypassed purchases. Current iPhone/iPad build 25 and Apple TV build 26 use the trial and lifetime access flow described above, including when delivered through TestFlight. Installing a TestFlight build does not itself grant a lifetime App Store purchase.

Cannot connect?

Wake the TV, open Open Brush once, and check that both devices are on the same network. Guest networks may isolate devices. On iPhone, check Local Network permission in Settings. If necessary, use TV settings → Forget this TV and pair again. Never share pairing codes or keys with support.

Video does not resume?

Picture in Picture and playback resumption depend on the other app and Apple TV software. Close overlays such as Control Center, start the main video, and try again. If needed, use the main app’s Play control. If another app stops the guide, choose Full Screen. You can also close a floating guide with the Siri Remote.

Contact support

Email support@openappco.com with the app version, device software versions, the step that failed, and what you saw. Do not include credentials, pairing codes, or a child’s personal information.

Open Brush is an educational demonstration, not diagnosis or treatment. The animation has not been reviewed by a dentist. An adult should supervise and follow their dentist’s individual advice.
