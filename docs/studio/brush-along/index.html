Open Brush puts a silent brushing guide in a small window beside a compatible video app. It shows which teeth to brush, which side, and how long is left.

Why it exists

Paul made this for his neurodivergent child, who for a long time would only brush while one particular video was playing. Turning the video off turned brushing into a fight.

So the video stays on. Open Brush sits in the corner of the screen and does the teaching: one surface at a time, the same order every night, no scores and no surprise sounds.

    How it works

    Get the toothbrush and toothpaste ready, with a grown-up nearby. Choose Full Screen for just the guide, or Picture in Picture to keep a compatible video playing beside it. The guide is silent, so your show keeps its sound.

    Apple TV, with the remote

    1. Start your show, then open Open Brush on your Apple TV box.
    2. Choose Picture in Picture. The guide gives you ten seconds to get ready.
    3. Double-click the remote’s TV button, choose your video app, and press Play if needed.

    The floating guide closes when the whole routine finishes. Choose Full Screen if your video app stops it.

    Watch on iPhone or iPad

    1. Launch Open Brush and choose Watch on this iPhone or iPad. You do not need an Apple TV.
    2. Choose Full Screen, or Picture in Picture for a floating guide.
    3. For Picture in Picture, return to your video app and press Play if needed.

    Practice & grown-up tips lets you replay a single surface with optional spoken instructions.

    Use your iPhone as the TV Start button

    1. Install Open Brush on your iPhone and Apple TV box. Use the same purchasing Apple Account and restore access on the other device if needed.
    2. Choose Set up my Apple TV on the phone. Keep both devices on the same home network, allow Local Network access, and enter the code shown on your TV.
    3. Play a show on the TV, then tap Start on Apple TV on your phone. Keep the phone app open until the guide starts and your show returns.

    Set up once; use the Start button next time. The Apple TV app built into a smart TV cannot replace an Apple TV streaming box. Get step-by-step setup help.

    The routine

    Six predictable sections: top outside, bottom outside, top inside, bottom inside, top chewing, then bottom chewing. The four outside and inside sections last 24 seconds each; the two chewing sections last 12 seconds each. That makes two full minutes on teeth.

    The whole film lasts 2 minutes 22 seconds: ten seconds to get ready, two minutes on teeth, eight extra seconds for a gentle tongue clean, and a four-second finish card. Teeth stay white; the see-through inside view helps reveal where the bristles go.

    A grown-up should help and check the back teeth. The timing is a teaching rhythm, not a medical claim, and the animation has not been reviewed by a dentist.

    Private by design
