Two minutes of brushing, floating over the show.

Brush Along puts a silent brushing guide in a small window over whatever your child is watching. It shows which teeth to brush, which side, and how long is left.

Why it exists

Paul made this for his neurodivergent child, who for a long time would only brush while one particular video was playing. Turning the video off turned brushing into a fight.

So the video stays on. Brush Along sits in the corner of the screen and does the teaching: one surface at a time, the same order every night, no scores and no surprise sounds.

    How it works

    Works alongside any video app. The guide has no sound, so the show keeps its own.

    Apple TV

    1. Open Brush Along and press Start.
    2. Double-click the TV button and pick your video app.
    3. Press play. The guide floats in the corner for two minutes, then closes itself.

    iPhone and iPad

    1. Open Brush Along and tap Try Watch & Brush.
    2. Tap the Picture in Picture button on the player.
    3. Switch to your video app. The guide stays on top while you watch.

    The app also has a narrated full screen coach and a way to practice any single step.

    The routine

    Four quarters of the mouth, 30 seconds each. In every quarter: 12 seconds on the outside, 12 on the inside, 6 on the chewing surfaces. The teeth to brush turn gold, and the picture is a mirror view, so it matches what your child sees in the bathroom mirror.

    A grown-up should help and check the back teeth. The timing is a teaching rhythm, not a medical claim, and the animation has not been reviewed by a dentist.
