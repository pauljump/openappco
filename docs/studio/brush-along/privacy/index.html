App privacy policy

Updated November 25, 2026

This policy covers the Open Brush app for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, viewing history, or personal information for its developer. It has no account system, advertisements, or tracking SDK.

Local playback and TV control

Brushing videos are bundled in the app and play offline. Optional iPhone control discovers and communicates with your Apple TV over your local network. Pairing credentials stay in your iPhone’s Keychain. Settings and bounded troubleshooting events stay on your device; the app does not upload them to us. Troubleshooting events omit pairing keys and viewing titles.

Apple purchases

Purchases and restoration are handled by Apple’s App Store. Open Brush checks Apple-verified transactions on your device to determine trial or lifetime access. We do not receive your payment details. Apple handles App Store information under its own privacy policy.

Your local data

You can remove a TV pairing in TV settings → Forget this TV. Removing the app deletes its local files; Keychain items can persist, so forget the TV before deleting the app if you also want to remove the saved pairing. Purchases remain associated with your Apple Account and can be restored. A trial does not restart after reinstalling.

Permissions and children

The app requests Local Network access only for TV control. No microphone, camera, contacts, location, or photo-library access is requested. Children can follow the guide with adult supervision; setup and purchases are for the grown-up.

If you contact support, include only the information you choose to share. Do not send pairing codes, credentials, or a child’s personal information.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@openappco.com. Open Brush is an Open AppCo app supported through this address.

Website hosting and support

The app policy above does not describe the separate Open AppCo website. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and handles technical connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement. Website tools are not part of the Open Brush app. Support emails contain the information you choose to send and are used to respond to your request.
