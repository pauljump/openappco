Brush Along for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV

Privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026.

Brush Along does not collect any information about you or your child. Nothing leaves your device.

In the app

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it makes no network requests. The brushing videos and narration are inside the app and play offline. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos, and it does not ask to track you across other apps.

On iPhone and iPad, the short spoken message at the end of the full screen coach is made by Apple's built in speech on the device. Brush Along does not keep a history of brushing sessions.

Children

Brush Along is made for families with young children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13, and nothing is shared with third parties. This is how we meet the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's rules for apps used by children.

This website

These Brush Along pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives standard request information such as your IP address under GitHub's privacy statement. The Brush Along pages load no analytics or cookies. Other pages on openappco.com use PostHog pageview and click analytics; those tools are not part of the app.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases. We do not receive your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
