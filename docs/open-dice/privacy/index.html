Open Dice for iPhone

Privacy policy

Open Dice contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, makes no network requests and has no Open AppCo server. It does not collect any information about you or your rolls.

On your iPhone

Open Dice stores three things in its own container on your iPhone: how many dice you use, whether the total is shown, and the list of rolls you've made (up to the last 10,000). Reset rolls clears the list. No account is needed. Deleting the app removes its data; iOS backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings.

Help websites

Support and Privacy links open your browser. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and receives technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement. The Open AppCo website uses PostHog pageview, click analytics and masked session replay; these website tools are not embedded in the iPhone app.

Contact

Use support@openappco.com or Open AppCo support for questions. GitHub issues are public.
