Open Tuner for iPhone

Help with Open Tuner

Open Tuner is a tuner for 6-string and 12-string guitar, ukulele, banjo and bass. This version has no account, ads or subscription, and every instrument and tuning is included.

Tune a string

Pick your instrument from the tabs, then a tuning from the bar under them. For a fixed manual target, turn off Tune by feel and, in About, Pick the string I play. Tap the peg for the string you want on the drawn headstock and pluck that string. The card shows the detected note, a meter and guidance. Tuned strings get a check.

Why does it say "Pluck again"?

If the reading could be an octave away from your target, Open Tuner asks for another pluck instead of a turning direction. Check the chosen tuning and peg before adjusting the string. A tuner reading cannot guarantee against string breakage.

Automatic selection

Pick the string I play, in About, follows the note you play. It's off at first. It does not advance just because a string is in tune. Tune by feel also enables automatic selection; turn both settings off to keep one manual target.

Hear the note

Tap Play to hear the target note once. Hold Play for 2 seconds to repeat it until you tap Stop; tap a peg while it repeats to switch strings. Pitch guidance pauses while the reference is playing. Reference notes work without microphone permission.

Returning to a string

Open Tuner remembers your selected string for each tuning. Leaving the app or locking the iPhone stops reference notes and vibration and clears the old reading. When you return, pluck again for a fresh measurement. Calls, interruptions and audio-device changes pause the tuner; tap Try microphone again to resume listening or Play to hear a reference. Reference notes do not restart automatically.

Tune by feel

On an iPhone with haptic support, Tune by feel follows the string you play and buzzes when it is in tune. Which way adds rising cues for low readings and falling cues for high readings. Try each pattern in About before using it with your instrument. The app must remain open; locking the iPhone or leaving the app stops vibration.

12-string

Each pair is tuned one string at a time. The low four pairs have an octave string (shown in lower case); the top two pairs are the same note.

Reference pitch

Tap the i button to set A4 from 432 to 446 Hz. Most music uses 440 Hz.

Microphone

Open Tuner needs the microphone to hear your instrument. If you said no, turn it on in Settings ▸ Open Tuner; the Play notes work without it. Music or a podcast keeps playing while you tune; if it plays out loud, the tuner may hear it too, so headphones help.
