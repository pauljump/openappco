Open Noise for iPhone

A little help with quiet.

Choose a sound and its level, then press Play. Add a second layer to make your own mix. Stop ends playback and cancels the timer.

Sleep timer

Open Timer & Settings for presets or a custom duration up to 24 hours. A timer chosen while stopped begins when you press Play. Replacing a running timer starts the new countdown immediately. Fade reduces only Open Noise’s sound during the final minute.

Calls and headphones

If a call interrupts playback or headphones disconnect, sound stays stopped until you choose Play again. An already running timer keeps its original end time. Open Noise does not change your iPhone’s system volume.

Offline and free

White, pink, and brown noise are generated continuously on your iPhone. Rain and ocean are included with the app. Nothing is recorded, downloaded, or transmitted while you listen. No account, advertisements, or payment is required.
