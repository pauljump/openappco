Open Beacon for iPhone

Privacy policy

Open Beacon has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking SDK. The native app does not send nearby-device observations or search activity to Open AppCo.

Bluetooth and location

The app listens for nearby Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements while it is open in the foreground. It stops scanning when you leave it or lock the phone. It does not connect to the devices it hears. The optional chirp uses an audio output already connected through iOS. Open Beacon does not request or use your location or microphone.

On your iPhone

Saved device identifiers, names, last-seen times and explicit pair choices are stored in the app’s settings on this iPhone. They are not uploaded to Open AppCo. You can clear a pair choice from Find and remove saved devices from Nearby. Deleting the app removes its local data; iOS backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings.

Help websites and support

Support and Privacy links open your browser when selected. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and receives technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. The Open AppCo website uses PostHog pageview, click analytics and masked session replay; these website tools are not embedded in the iPhone app. If you email support, we receive your message and email address so we can respond.

Contact

Email support@openappco.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Please leave out sensitive personal information. You can also use Open AppCo support; GitHub issues are public.
