Open Beacon for iPhone

Help with Open Beacon

Open Beacon follows one nearby Bluetooth Low Energy device through vibration, a large proximity word and optional speech. The device must broadcast a detectable signal. This version has no account, ads or in-app purchase.

Find something

Open the app, read the limits and allow Bluetooth. Saved things are listed first, followed by nearby devices. Choose the device you want to find and walk slowly. Far, Closer, Near, Very near and Right here describe its smoothed signal; the trend says whether that signal is getting stronger or weaker. These are not distance measurements. Walls, bodies, pockets and device behavior affect the reading.

Keep the selected target

If the selected device stops advertising, Find says Not heard. Another device does not take over the search. With Speak on, speech or VoiceOver announces the signal state and signal loss. Vibration is available on supported hardware. Keep Open Beacon in the foreground; this app stops scanning when you leave it or lock the phone. The screen stays awake while you search.

One of a pair

If a pair appears as two devices, first choose the lost member. Open Wearing the other one? and identify the device you know you are wearing. Names suggest a pair; signal strength cannot identify which member you are wearing. The app remembers your choice. Use Change pair choice to reverse or clear it. A remembered pair remains in Your things or Remembered pairs even when the lost member is silent.

Save and rename

Long press a device, or use its VoiceOver actions, to save it and give it your own name. Saved devices show when this iPhone last heard them. This is a time, not a saved location.

Play a sound

Play sound is available for a matching Bluetooth audio output already connected to this iPhone. The chirp may play through both earpieces; pair selection does not mute either one. Remove headphones or hearing aids before playing it. If the output changes or sound fails to start, check the connection and choose Play sound again.

Compatibility and limits

Open Beacon does not use the Find My network, a map or directional ranging. It cannot guarantee finding a device that is switched off, out of battery, out of range or closed in its case. Earbud and hearing-aid compatibility depends on detectable Bluetooth advertisements. For supported Apple devices, also check Find My.
