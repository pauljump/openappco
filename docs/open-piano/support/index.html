Open Piano for iPhone

Help with Open Piano

A straightforward piano with local recording and playback.

Contact support

Email pauljump@me.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Does it need an internet connection?

Playing, recording, and playback work offline. The Wishlist needs a connection to fetch updates; a previously loaded list can remain available offline.

Suggest a change

Join the Open AppCo discussion or use the existing Wishlist. GitHub posts are public and require a GitHub account to participate. You can always email us instead.
