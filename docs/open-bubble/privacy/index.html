Open Bubble for iPhone

Privacy policy

Open Bubble contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, makes no network requests and has no Open AppCo server. It does not collect any information about you or how you play.

On your iPhone

Open Bubble stores the current scene, your session progress and your settings in its own container on your iPhone. No account is needed. Deleting the app removes its data; iOS backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings.

Help websites

Support and Privacy links open your browser. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and receives technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement. The Open AppCo website uses PostHog pageview, click analytics and masked session replay; these website tools are not embedded in the iPhone app.

Contact

Use support@openappco.com or Open AppCo support for questions. GitHub issues are public.
