Keep the good stuff.

A little space for who you are now.

Prune helps you review your own Facebook, Instagram and X history, a few posts at a time. Import the download each platform gives you, decide what stays, and save the rest to a review queue.

Every platform is included. No subscription, in-app purchase or Prune account.

Your history. Your account.

  1. Request your archive. Open the platform’s app or your browser, using the account already signed in there. Return to Prune when the download is ready. You do not need developer credentials or API keys.
  2. Review on your phone. Import the unzipped archive from Files. Your goal puts matching posts first; you make every decision.
  3. Make each change yourself. Prune opens the original in the platform’s app or your browser. Use its controls, check the result, then return to Prune and mark it complete.

The App Store version does not embed social websites, scrape your timeline or automatically delete posts. Preparing an archive can take time, and supported archive contents vary by platform.

How to get started

No app data sent to Open AppCo.

Archives, goals and review decisions stay in the app on your device. Prune has no app server, advertising or analytics SDK. Your social logins stay in your social apps or browser, outside Prune. Imported media is displayed locally; Prune does not fetch remote images.
