Keep the good stuff.

A little space for who you are now.

Prune helps you review your own social history in small, untimed sessions. Keep, Hide or Delete saves a local decision. Your progress stays on your phone while you make changes using the platform’s own controls.

Every supported platform and review session is free. No subscription, in-app purchase, Prune account or developer API key.

Your history. Your account.

  1. Start with a guide to the platform’s own cleanup tools. Open its app or your browser and use your usual social-account login. You can use these guides without importing an archive.
  2. Optionally request an archive from the platform’s official download tool, save and unzip it in Files, then import the supported records into Prune. Review a few posts and keep your decisions. Imports are not a complete copy of every kind of account data.
  3. Open the platform from your queue and make changes yourself. Use Facebook’s Activity Log or Instagram’s Your Activity for available bulk controls; on X, handle posts individually. Check the result before returning to Prune and marking an item complete.

Prune does not read your live timeline or operate platform controls. It cannot inspect which account is signed into another app or browser or independently verify a deletion. Platform features and recovery rules vary. Keep your original archive before changing posts.

How to get started

Local review, your platform’s tools.

Your imported posts, goal and review decisions stay in Prune’s files on your iPhone. Prune has no application backend, advertising or analytics SDK, or remote AI service. It opens social links in the platform’s app or your browser; that service handles its own login and data collection. Prune does not receive those passwords or session cookies.

Media previews come from supported local imported files. They are not a complete carousel, album or video backup. Photos saving supports primary local imported images. Keep the full original archive separately.
