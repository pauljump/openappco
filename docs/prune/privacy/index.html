Prune for iPhone

Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026. This policy covers the Prune iPhone app’s archive and manual-review workflow.

What Open AppCo collects through the app

Open AppCo does not collect your posts, archive, goal, review decisions, social-account credentials or usage analytics through Prune. The app has no Prune account, application server, advertising, tracking SDK or remote AI service. Every platform is free, with no in-app purchases or subscription.

What stays on your device

Imported post records, available media, your goal, recommendations and decisions are stored in the app’s local files. Optional topic labeling uses Apple’s on-device model when available. Prune’s browser stores website sessions and website data on the device so you can use your own social accounts. Prune does not ask you to paste passwords, session cookies or API keys into its own controls.

Your device or cloud backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Original downloads saved in Files and copies saved to Photos are separate from Prune’s local records.

Social websites and their services

When you choose to open Facebook, Instagram or X, Prune displays the platform’s own website using Apple’s WebKit browser. You enter credentials on that website. The platform receives information you submit and ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and browser requests, under its own privacy policy.

Those pages may load platform media, content delivery networks, sign-in providers and security or login-challenge services. They may store cookies and other website data. These requests go directly from your device to those services, not through an Open AppCo server. Prune’s browser is restricted to supported platform and supporting-service domains; this does not prevent those services from processing information when you use them.

The App Store workflow imports official downloads and opens pages you choose to use. It does not scrape timelines, automatically operate platform controls or automatically verify remote deletion.

Your choices

You can review imported records without signing in to a platform. Sign-in is needed only for the platform’s own download or account actions. Use Erase everything Prune knows to clear Prune’s local records and saved website sessions on this device. It does not delete social posts, original archives in Files or photos you saved to Photos. Manage device backups through your Apple settings.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send so we can respond. Do not send passwords or private archives. We do not sell this information.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes technical requests under GitHub’s privacy statement. Open AppCo websites use PostHog for page visits, clicks and masked session replay. Website analytics are separate from the native Prune app and do not receive your imported archive or in-app decisions.
