Prune for iPhone

Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026. This policy covers the Prune iPhone app’s archive and manual-review workflow, which opens social services in their own apps or your default browser.

What Open AppCo collects through the app

Open AppCo does not collect your posts, archive, goal, review decisions, social-account credentials or usage analytics through Prune. The app has no Prune account, application server, advertising, tracking SDK or remote AI service. Every platform is free, with no in-app purchases or subscription.

What stays on your device

Imported post records, available media, your goal, recommendations and decisions are stored in the app’s local files. Optional topic labeling uses Apple’s on-device model when available. Prune does not embed social websites, store their login sessions or ask you to paste passwords, session cookies or API keys. It displays local imported media without downloading remote images.

Your device or cloud backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Original downloads saved in Files and copies saved to Photos are separate from Prune’s local records.

Social websites and their services

When you choose to open Facebook, Instagram or X, Prune asks iOS to open the link in the platform’s own app or your default browser. Your existing login stays there. Prune cannot inspect the account signed into another app or browser, and does not receive its credentials or cookies. The external service receives information you submit and ordinary connection information under its own privacy policy.

The external social apps and websites may use media hosts, content delivery networks, sign-in providers, cookies and security services. That activity occurs outside Prune. Their privacy policies and your app/browser settings govern it; Prune’s no-collection statement does not describe those independent services.

The App Store workflow imports official downloads and opens external links you choose to use. It does not scrape timelines, automatically operate platform controls or automatically verify remote deletion.

Your choices

You can review imported records without signing in to a platform. Sign-in for a download or account action happens only in the external platform app or browser. Use Erase everything Prune knows to clear Prune’s local app records on this device. It does not sign you out of social apps or Safari, delete social posts, remove original archives in Files or remove photos you saved to Photos. Manage device backups through your Apple settings.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send so we can respond. Do not send passwords or private archives. We do not sell this information.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes technical requests under GitHub’s privacy statement. Open AppCo websites use PostHog for page visits, clicks and masked session replay. Website analytics are separate from the native Prune app and do not receive your imported archive or in-app decisions.
