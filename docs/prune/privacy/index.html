Prune for iPhone

Privacy policy

Effective November 28, 2026.

This policy covers Prune’s public-development workflow: local archive review, saved decisions and links to social services in their own apps or your browser. Prune is not yet on the App Store. Supported archive formats and the final release are still being tested.

What Open AppCo receives through the native app

Prune has no application backend or Prune account. It does not upload your imported social history, goals or review decisions to Open AppCo. The app includes no advertising or analytics SDK and does not send content to a remote AI service. This describes the native app’s own services; social apps and websites you open receive their own requests and information.

What stays on your iPhone

Prune saves supported posts and media you import, your goal and your review decisions in local app files. Optional topic labeling uses Apple’s on-device model when available. Media previews use local imported files; the public workflow does not fetch remote social images. Your device or cloud backups may include these files according to your Apple settings. Your original archives in Files and independent Photos copies are separate.

Your social-account sign-in

Prune opens social links in the platform’s own app or your browser. Sign-in and verification happen there, using your usual account. Prune does not embed the social website, read your live timeline, inspect its signed-in account, receive passwords or session cookies, or ask for developer API keys. It cannot use a login in another app to perform actions for you.

What social services receive

The app or website you open receives information you submit and ordinary connection information such as your IP address. It may use cookies, media hosts, sign-in/security services, advertising and other service providers. Their collection, retention and uses depend on the service and your settings. Prune’s local review does not make these separate services collection-free.

Read the applicable Facebook/Meta privacy policy, Instagram privacy policy and X privacy policy. Their data practices are independent of Open AppCo. Prune does not claim to prevent tracking in another app or browser.

Your choices and local erasure

You can use the made-up demo or review supported imports without signing into a social account. Use Erase everything Prune knows to clear Prune’s saved local library. If Prune reports an erasure error, retry before assuming the affected local files are gone.

Local erasure does not delete social posts or your social account, clear information retained by the platform, log you out of Safari or other apps, remove original archives saved outside Prune, remove independent Photos copies or erase device backups. Use the platform’s controls for its account data and Apple settings for backups.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send so we can respond. Do not send passwords, cookies, authentication tokens or private archives. Remove personal details from screenshots you choose to share. Prune does not automatically upload your archive to support.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes technical requests under GitHub’s privacy statement. Open AppCo websites use PostHog for page visits, clicks and masked session replay. Website analytics are separate from the native app and do not receive your imported archive or local review decisions from Prune.

For privacy questions or requests about information you sent to support, contact support@openappco.com.
