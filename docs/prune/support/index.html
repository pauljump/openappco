Prune for iPhone · In development

Prune support

Prune is a free iPhone app being prepared for the App Store. There are no purchases or subscriptions. It is not yet publicly available to download. Supported archive formats and the final release are still being tested.

Use your usual social account

Prune opens social links in the platform’s app or your browser. Use the account already signed in there, or sign in there with your usual login and verification. Prune does not receive that login. No Prune account or developer API key is required. The cleanup guides are available without importing an archive. The made-up demo lets you try local review without signing in.

Import an official archive

Request your information through the platform’s official download tool. Save its archive in Files, unzip it and choose Import your archive folder… in Prune. Supported JSON post records and available local media are imported; HTML-only exports and unsupported records may not appear. Keep the full original archive as a separate backup.

Review a few posts at a time

Your goal orders the pile and suggests what deserves attention. Keep, Hide or Delete records a local decision; a swipe does not change the platform. Use Undo if you change your mind. Your saved decisions let you pause and return later.

Use the platform’s cleanup tools

Open a queued item or the platform from Prune. Check the account and the posts before making changes yourself. Facebook’s Activity Log and Instagram’s Your Activity offer native bulk controls for supported content. On X, delete posts individually using its own controls. Availability varies by platform and content type; follow the options shown in your account.

Prune does not click controls, read the live page or confirm the outcome. After you inspect the result yourself, return to Prune and mark the item complete. This records what you report, not independent platform verification. If the result is uncertain, check the platform before retrying. Deletion and recovery rules vary, and some changes are permanent.

Keep your media separately

Prune displays supported local imported images. A preview is not a complete carousel, album, video or full-resolution backup. Photos saving supports primary local imported image files. Keep and check your original archive before deleting a post.

Sign-in or an old post is not working

Complete security challenges in the platform’s own app or browser. Check that you are using the right account. An archive may contain an old or missing link; use the platform’s own history or activity tools to find the post. Prune cannot guarantee that every original is still available. Stop rather than marking an uncertain action complete.

Remove your Prune data

Use Erase everything Prune knows to clear Prune’s saved local records. It does not delete platform posts, sign you out of other apps or Safari, or remove original archives and independent Photos copies. Device backups follow your Apple settings. If an erasure error appears, retry before treating local removal as complete.

Get help

Email support@openappco.com with the app/build version, platform, iOS version and a brief description. Do not send passwords, cookies, tokens, API keys or your entire archive. Remove private details from any screenshot you choose to share.
