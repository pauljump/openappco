Prune support

Prune is being prepared for the App Store. It is free, with no purchases or subscription. It is not yet publicly available to download.

Start with your own account

Choose Facebook, Instagram or X, then open its download page. Sign in on that platform’s own page with your usual login, passkey and any two-factor verification. You do not create a Prune account or paste an API key into the app.

Import your history

  1. Request your information from the platform’s official download tool. For Facebook and Instagram, select your profile, all time, JSON format and the information you want to review. For X, request your account archive.
  2. Wait for the platform to prepare the download. Save the ZIP to Files on your iPhone and tap it to unzip it.
  3. In Prune, choose the platform, tap Import a download…, and select the unzipped folder.

If no posts appear, check that you chose the unzipped folder and JSON rather than an HTML-only export. Imports cover supported post records, not every kind of data in an archive. Keep your original archive as your backup.

Review a few posts at a time

Your goal orders the pile and suggests what deserves attention. Keep, Hide or Delete saves your decision locally; a swipe does not change the platform. Use Undo if you change your mind. You can pause and return to your saved decisions.

Apply changes on the platform

Open the bin and work through your queue. Prune opens the original post on the platform’s own page. Check that you are signed into the correct account, make the change using its controls, and confirm the result yourself before marking it complete in Prune. Prune does not automatically click controls or verify that a remote post was deleted.

Deletion and recovery depend on the platform and content type. X deletions can be permanent. Keep a separate archive and read the platform’s confirmation carefully.

Sign-in or a post is not working

Complete any security check on the platform’s page. If it is unavailable, try later or open the platform directly in Safari or its own app. An archive can contain an old or missing link; Prune cannot guarantee that every original is still available. Do not mark an action complete unless you checked it.

Remove your Prune data

Use Erase everything Prune knows to clear Prune’s local records and saved website sessions on this device. This does not delete posts from your social accounts. Archives you saved in Files and photos saved to Photos remain under your control. Device backups follow your Apple settings.

Get help

Email support@openappco.com with the platform, iOS version and what happened. Do not send passwords, session cookies, API keys or your entire archive. Remove personal details from any screenshot you choose to share.
