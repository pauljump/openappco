Open Flashcards for iPhone

Privacy policy

Open Flashcards contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not collect or upload your decks, cards or study activity.

On your iPhone

Decks, cards and active study state are stored locally in the app’s container. No account, network connection or device permission is required for the core app. Deleting the app removes its local data; iOS backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings.

Files you choose

CSV import uses the system file picker. CSV export uses the system share sheet. The app accesses only the file you select or creates the export you request. Once you save or share an export, the chosen destination’s privacy practices apply.

Help websites

Support and Privacy links open your browser. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and receives technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. The Open AppCo website uses PostHog pageview, click analytics and masked session replay; these website tools are not embedded in the iPhone app and do not receive local flashcard content.

Contact

Use support@openappco.com or Open AppCo support for questions. GitHub issues are public; do not post private study material.
