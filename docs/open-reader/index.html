What we reviewed

On October 12, 2026, we captured 6,760 unique public review entries across 11 source apps (4 from discovery and 7 direct ebook and comic readers); 3,186 were rated one to three stars. The audit requested pages 1–10 of Apple's most-recent and most-helpful feeds in the United States, Canada, Great Britain and Australia. All 329 requested feed snapshots succeeded.

Apple caps these public feeds at ten pages. Four source apps filled page 10, so older reviews may exist beyond what Apple exposed. This is every verified complaint category Open Reader claims to address—not every review ever written.
