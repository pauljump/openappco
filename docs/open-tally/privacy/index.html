Open Tally for iPhone

Privacy policy

Open Tally contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not upload your counters.

Your counts

Counter names, values, colors, and preferences are stored on your iPhone. Counting works without an internet connection. Resetting a count or deleting a counter requires confirmation. Deleting the app removes its local data; device backups are managed by Apple and your device settings.

The Bench

Opening The Bench in Settings downloads public community updates from pauljump.github.io/openappco. The app does not send counter names, values, an account, or an app-specific user identifier. The client does not accept or set cookies. The service receives the technical information needed for an internet request, including an IP address in transit. A cached feed may remain available offline.

Websites and support

Links open websites in your browser. GitHub Pages hosts these help pages; its handling of technical request information is covered by GitHub’s privacy statement. These Open Tally help pages do not embed analytics scripts. The Open Tally Bench is a static GitHub Pages page with no analytics, voting cookies, or payment backend. If you post a suggestion on GitHub it is public. If you email support, we receive your message and email address so we can respond. Please do not put private counter information in public issues.
