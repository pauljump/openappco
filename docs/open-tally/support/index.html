Open Tally for iPhone

A little help with counting.

Open Tally opens ready to use. Tap + to add one and − to subtract one. Every change is saved on this iPhone.

Make it yours

Tap the + in the top corner to make a named counter. Choose a starting value and a color. Open a counter’s three-dot menu to rename it or enter a whole number, including a negative number.

Focus and arrange

Tap “Focus on this counter” for a larger view. In Settings, choose how to sort counters. Choose Custom and use Edit on the board to put them in your own order.

Reset and delete

Reset returns a count to zero and keeps its name and color. Delete removes the whole counter. Both ask you to confirm first. Settings also lets you reset every count at once.

Your saved counts

Counts stay on this iPhone and are not synced to other devices. If the app shows a recovery message, check the restored values. If it cannot open saved data, it keeps the original data intact: contact support before deleting or reinstalling the app.

Feedback

Turn haptic feedback on or off in Settings. The Bench shows proposed improvements when you have an internet connection. Counting always works offline.

Get in touch

Email pauljump@me.com or visit the Open Tally Bench. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Please leave private counter information out of support messages.
