Open Breath for iPhone

Privacy policy

Open Breath contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not collect or upload your breathing sessions.

On your iPhone

Preferences and the latest 100 completed sessions are stored locally. No account, HealthKit permission or server is required. Clear local history from the History screen. Deleting the app removes app data; iOS backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings.

Help websites

Support and Privacy links open your browser. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and receives technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. The Open AppCo website uses PostHog pageview, click analytics and masked session replay; these website tools are not embedded in the iPhone app and do not receive local breathing history.

Contact

Use Open AppCo support for questions. GitHub issues are public; do not post private health or session information.
