Open Breath for iPhone

A little space.

Open Breath is a free breathing timer for iPhone. Choose a pattern and session length, then tap Begin.

Your rhythm

Choose Box, Settle, 4–7–8 or custom timing in Settings. Holds can be zero seconds. Timed sessions finish their current full cycle, so the session can run slightly beyond the selected time.

Pause and finish

Pause and Resume keep your place. Finish this breath saves after the current complete cycle. End now stops immediately without saving the unfinished session. Locking the phone or leaving the app pauses practice.

Sound and touch

Settings has optional chimes and haptic cues. Chimes follow silent mode. Keep the app open while practicing. Breathe comfortably; this is a general wellness timer, not medical treatment.

Your history

The latest 100 completed sessions stay on your iPhone. History → Clear asks for confirmation before deleting them.

Contact

Report a problem on GitHub with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Issues are public; do not include private health or session information.
