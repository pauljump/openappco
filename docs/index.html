Why this exists

A kid hunting for a melody doesn’t need an upsell.

The kid wanted to find a song by ear. Tap a key, hear a note, find the next one.

The apps we tried kept stopping the music for ads. Other people noticed too. One review of a piano app says, “literally every single time I go to hit a key an ad pops up.” Another says, “Now you can’t close the ad to get back to the keyboard.”

So we built Open Piano: labeled keys, a trail of the notes you played, and record and play back. It has no ads, no account, and no tracking.

Then we looked around. We collected 121,602 App Store reviews and went through the 46,070 one- and two-star ones, across 2,540 apps. We found the same thing everywhere. The tuner wants your personal data. The tally counter “demands $13 every time I open the app.” The white-noise machine stops after five minutes unless you pay. Small tools that each do one job, buried under ads and paywalls.

Before we build one, we pull every review Apple’s public feeds show for the apps it replaces, and the complaints that keep coming up become its requirements and tests. Those feeds are capped, so we say what we could and couldn’t see. See how the audit works.

We build them one at a time.

Heard in one-star reviews

Why this matters

A simple tool should just work.

The people who need simple tools most get hit hardest.

Kids. Grandparents with a magnifier. Someone logging blood pressure for a doctor. They’re the least able to dodge a dark pattern, and the most likely to tap the wrong button on a $9.99/week trial.

These apps cost almost nothing to run.

A piano doesn’t need a server. A tally counter doesn’t need your email. Once it’s built well, one more person using it costs us close to zero. A weekly subscription for one makes no sense.

Your data should outlive the app.

People lose years of journal entries and health history to an update or a sudden paywall. Our apps keep everything on your phone and export it in one tap. If you leave, your data goes with you.

  • No ads. Not now, not “later.”
  • No account. Open it and it works.
  • No tracking. What you do stays on your phone.
  • Your data exports. Plain files, one tap.
  • No tricks. No fake countdowns, no surprise trials.

We don’t think little apps like these stay a business for anyone for long. They’re headed into whatever a few big AI companies put on your phone next. We’d like them to be good, and kind, while they’re still apps.
